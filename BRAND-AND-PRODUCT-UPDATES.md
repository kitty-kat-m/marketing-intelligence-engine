# Field & Compass Travel — fictional prototype brand

Entirely synthetic. Inspired by general adventure-travel experience, not Intrepid records, branding or confidential information.

## Brand
Small-group cultural, nature and walking trips for curious adults. Lead with specific experiences, local knowledge and practical detail. Voice: warm, informed, inviting and direct. Avoid superlatives, invented testimonials, cultural stereotypes and unsubstantiated environmental claims. Do not describe a trip as accessible, safe for everyone or carbon neutral without evidence.

Illustrative market: English-speaking US travellers. Currency: USD. These are prototype assumptions to change later. Social channels: Instagram and Facebook. Email audience: opted-in prospects grouped by interests, with no actual personal data in this pack.

## Planning defaults
Plan November 2026, using information available as of 18 October 2026. Propose three social concepts per week, each adapted to Instagram and Facebook. Propose one email per week, with up to two in sale week. Frequency is an editable setting, not an industry recommendation. Space campaigns across channels rather than repeating identical copy everywhere.

Priority: fixed sales commitments first; product launches next; evidence-backed product opportunities next; relevant seasonal and trend ideas fill remaining slots. Never promote a sold-out departure or a product before its launch. Do not invent event dates or call a synthetic signal a real trend. Major sports and film events are examples of future themes, not verified November fixtures.

## Email output requirements
Export subject, preview text, plain text and HTML separately. HTML should use a simple responsive, table-based structure with inline styles, descriptive links and image alt text. Include an editable footer with the real company's address and the selected email platform's unsubscribe token before use. No active links or tracking are supplied: use example.com and clearly identify placeholders. A marketer must preview and test in the eventual email platform; pasteable HTML alone does not establish compatibility.

## Two approval stages
Calendar approval covers dates, audience, channel, topic, products, offers, evidence and rationale. Content approval covers exact wording, subject, preview text, links, creative brief and email HTML. Changing an approved calendar entry invalidates its generated content and both approvals for that entry. Changing generated content invalidates content approval. Changing relevant inputs marks affected entries stale for review. Export only current, approved content; never send or publish automatically.
